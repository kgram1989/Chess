The piece images in this folder are third-party artwork, taken from the open-source
Lichess repository (https://github.com/lichess-org/lila, `public/piece/`).
Each set stays under its own licence, listed below.

## What this means in practice

- **Staunty and Maestro are non-commercial.** They may not be used in anything sold or
  monetised, and any adapted versions must be shared under the same licence, with credit.
  Fine for a personal game; remove these two folders (and their menu entries) before any
  commercial release.
- **Cburnett and Merida are GPLv2+.** Redistributing them requires keeping this notice and
  the licence terms with the files.
- The images are unmodified copies of the originals.
